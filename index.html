FAQs

Everything you need to know about the Liquid Network and SideSwap.

The Liquid Network is a Bitcoin sidechain designed to enable fast, confidential, and secure asset transfers. It serves as a financial layer for Bitcoin, allowing users and institutions to issue, transfer, and trade a variety of digital assets—including Bitcoin itself—on a network where Bitcoin acts as the native settlement currency. Liquid enhances privacy through confidential transactions and supports rapid settlement, making it ideal for exchanges, traders, and asset issuers seeking efficiency and discretion.
Assets can be issued as “bearer” or Transfer Restricted (called AMP assets). Bearer assets may be held and transferred without permission. AMP assets may only be transferred if the issuer doesn’t withhold their permission.
AMP assets allow for the creation of decentralized securities registry where the user maintains custody and control over the asset while the issuer may track ownership.
